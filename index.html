Features

Everything a serious decision-maker needs

Reflect OS is not a note-taking app or a task manager. It's infrastructure for decisions — built around the closed loop of capture, review, and learn.

Decision log

Every decision captured with structured fields: context, rationale, risks, confidence, stakeholders, and situational notes. Fast to complete — nothing gets lost.

Outcome checkpoints

Set review milestones at 30, 90, 180 days or custom horizons. Reflect OS surfaces them at the right time — so outcomes are reviewed, not forgotten.

Decision intelligence

Calibration curves, quality scores, and pattern detection across your history. The app earns trust by showing you something true about how you actually decide.

Team workspaces

Share decisions with your team. IC votes, collaborative review, shareable decision briefs via secure expiring links — for non-users too.

AI risk assessment

Every decision gets a structured risk analysis. Surface what you might have missed, scored by severity and confidence, with mitigation suggestions.

Financial modelling toolkit

Run projections and scenario analysis within the decision record. Outputs stay linked to the decision that created them — not buried in a spreadsheet.

Security & privacy

Your reasoning is yours alone

Decisions often contain your most sensitive thinking. Reflect OS is built around the premise that what's private stays private — without compromising what the AI needs to work.

Full security details

End-to-end encryption for sensitive fields

Decision rationale and sensitive context fields are encrypted at rest. Not readable by anyone without your key.

Granular access controls

Workspace, team, and individual permissions. You decide who can see what — including coaches and advisors.

Full audit log

Every view, edit, and export is logged. Defensible records for governance and compliance.
