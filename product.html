Product

Decision infrastructure from first log to final learning

Everything in Reflect OS is built around the closed loop: structured capture at the moment of decision, scheduled outcome review, and pattern intelligence over time.

Decision log

Structured capture of context, rationale, confidence, risks, stakeholders, and situational notes. Fast to complete, impossible to lose.

Outcome checkpoints

Schedule reviews at 30, 90, 180 days, or custom horizons. Compare what you expected with what happened — at exactly the right moment.

Intelligence dashboard

Calibration curve, quality score trends, bias detection, and pattern analysis across your full decision history.

AI risk assessment

Structured risk analysis with severity scoring and mitigation suggestions. Surface what you might have missed before committing.

Scenario and assumption tools

Projections, scenarios, and cashflow models — linked to the decision that generated them, not buried in a spreadsheet.

Team workspaces

IC voting, shared decision review, granular permissions. Shareable briefs for external stakeholders via secure expiring links.

Why not a spreadsheet or Notion?

Spreadsheets and general-purpose tools like Notion are excellent at storage. They are not built for the closed loop. They don't prompt you when an outcome is due. They don't track your confidence calibration over time. They don't surface the pattern that you're consistently overconfident in a specific category of decision. They don't generate AI risk assessments before you commit. And they don't produce a defensible audit trail when governance requires one.

Reflect OS is infrastructure. It does one thing — and the one thing it does, nothing else does.
