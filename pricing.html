FAQ

Common questions

Is there a get started?

There is no free trial. All plans come with a 90-day money-back guarantee — if Reflect OS isn’t right for you, contact us within 90 days for a full refund.

Can I switch between Individual and Team?

Yes. Upgrade to Team at any time. All your decisions, checkpoints, intelligence, and history carry over with no data loss.

What counts as a user on the Team plan?

Any person with their own login to your workspace. You can share decision briefs via secure expiring links with external stakeholders at no extra cost — they don't need an account.

How is my data stored and protected?

Decision rationale and sensitive fields are encrypted at rest. We do not use your decision data for model training. Full details are on our security page.

What happens if I cancel?

You retain full access until the end of your billing period. You can export all your decisions, checkpoints, and records at any time before or after cancelling.
