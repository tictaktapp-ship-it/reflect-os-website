5. AI providers and your data

AI-assisted features in Reflect OS are powered by Anthropic Claude (models including Claude 3.5 Haiku and Claude 3.5 Sonnet). When you use an AI feature, relevant portions of your decision data (such as the decision description and context) are sent to Anthropic's API for processing.

Anthropic does not use data submitted via the API to train its AI models. Data sent to the Anthropic API is processed only to return the requested output and is not stored by Anthropic beyond the period required to generate the response, in accordance with Anthropic's API data usage policy.

We process your data when sending it to Anthropic on the basis of our contract with you (providing the AI features you have requested). You may disable AI features at any time via Settings → AI Features, in which case no data is sent to Anthropic.

8. Data deletion in the app

You can manage and delete your data directly within the Service at any time:

  • Export your data: Settings → Data & Privacy → Export — downloads all your decisions, outcomes, and records in a portable format.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete Account — permanently closes your account and initiates deletion of your personal data within 30 days.
  • Delete individual decisions: Any decision can be deleted from within the decision detail view.

9. Security

We take the security of your data seriously and implement appropriate technical and organisational measures, including:

  • All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher.
  • Sensitive fields (decision descriptions, context, outcomes, coaching notes) are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM via Supabase Vault.
  • Authentication tokens are stored securely and rotated regularly.
  • Full audit logging is maintained for account and workspace actions.
  • Access to production systems is restricted to authorised personnel only.
  • In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the ICO within 72 hours and notify affected users without undue delay.

No system is completely secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, please contact us immediately at contact@reflect-os.com.
