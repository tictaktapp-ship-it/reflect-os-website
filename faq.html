Decision intelligence

What is a decision intelligence platform?

A decision intelligence platform is software designed to help individuals and organisations systematically track, review, and improve their decision-making. Core capabilities include decision logging, outcome tracking, confidence calibration analysis, and review scheduling. Reflect OS is a decision intelligence platform focused on executives and investment teams. Full definition →

What is confidence calibration?

Confidence calibration measures how accurately your stated confidence predicts your actual outcomes. A well-calibrated decision-maker who says "I am 70% confident" succeeds 70% of the time across a large sample of such decisions. Reflect OS calculates your calibration score automatically from your decision records. Full guide to confidence calibration →

How does the 30/90/180-day review cadence work?

When you log a decision in Reflect OS, you choose a review horizon — typically 30, 90, or 180 days, depending on how long outcomes take to become visible. At that date, Reflect OS sends you a review prompt with your original decision record: what you decided, why, and your confidence level. You then record the actual outcome and the gap between expectation and reality. Over time, these reviews build your calibration dataset.

How many decisions do I need before calibration data is meaningful?

Calibration patterns typically become visible after 30–50 reviewed decisions in a single category. Below that threshold, the sample is too small to distinguish signal from noise. At 100+ decisions, calibration data is actionable enough to identify the specific decision types where your confidence is systematically miscalibrated.
